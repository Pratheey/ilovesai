# I LOVE SAI — Website Content Brief

**What this is:** every piece of text and every photo the website is still waiting on, written as plain questions. The site is built and working; these are the blanks.

**How to use it:** go through it in conversation. Answer in fragments — bullet points, half-sentences, "ask Ramesh about this" — nothing needs to be polished. Rough notes get turned into finished website copy afterwards. Skip anything you don't know and move on.

**Two rules that matter:**

1. **Nothing gets invented.** If nobody knows the answer, it stays blank. A blank is fine; a made-up detail on a devotional site is not.
2. **For anything about Baba's life, Shirdi, or his teachings — give the source, not the memory.** Which book, which chapter. Those sections are marked "Source needed" below.

**★ = do these first.** They're the ones a visitor sees in the first ten seconds. About fifteen answers make the whole site feel real.

## What is NOT being asked for

So the conversation doesn't drift into these:

- Anything about how the group runs internally. WhatsApp and Excel work, and the website deliberately doesn't try to replace them.
- A membership form. Newcomers go to the coordinator on WhatsApp, and the existing form stays where it is, downstream.
- Design opinions, colours or layout. That's settled and built.
- Translations. Hindi and Marathi are possible later — the site was built ready for them — but that's a separate decision, not part of this.
